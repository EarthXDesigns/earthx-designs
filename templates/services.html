Solar Engineering Services

Comprehensive design and engineering packages tailored for residential installers, commercial developers, and utility EPC firms.

Solar 3D Design

We create highly detailed 3D models of homes, commercial complexes, and industrial sites. This allows developers to present professional, photorealistic installations to clients, securing sales faster.

Solar 2D Layout Design

Accurate AutoCAD layout blueprints highlighting solar panel orientations, row pitch, setback boundary lines, walkways, and inverter station placements optimized for maintenance access.

Shadow Analysis

We run full yearly solar path simulations to identify shading caused by chimneys, trees, AC units, and parapet walls. Helps determine string routing and structural setbacks to minimize performance loss.

SLD Drawings

Electrical Single Line Diagrams detailing system specifications, string mapping, fuse/breaker ratings, wire sizes, grounding systems, and disconnect switch placements for rapid utility permit approvals.

BOQ Preparation

Detailed Bills of Quantities accounting for every PV module, rail, clamp, cable, connector, inverter, and combiner box needed. Prevents purchasing errors and assists in budget estimations.

CEIG Drawings

Approval drawings customized for Chief Electrical Inspector regulatory agencies. Includes detailed grounding grid schematics, lightning arrester layouts, and substation layouts.

Ground Mount Solar Design

Civil and structural designs for large-scale utility farms. We handle slope analysis, tracker layouts, foundation pile depth modeling, and structural load calculations for extreme wind zones.

Rooftop Solar Design

Optimized residential and commercial rooftop solar layouts. Designs are structured to evenly distribute loads, avoid roof penetrations where possible, and maximize energy density per square foot.

Solar Engineering Consultancy

Expert advisory on solar tech selection, feasibility analysis, cable loss mitigation, grid integration layouts, and cost-reduction strategies for major commercial and utility developments.

Need a Custom Design Package?

We provide customized monthly engineering retainer contracts for high-volume installers, as well as pay-per-project designs.
