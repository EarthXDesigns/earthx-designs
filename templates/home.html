Our Expertise

Premium Engineering Services

We support every stage of the solar design pipeline, from initial feasibility studies to final permitting and electrical single line diagrams.

Solar 3D Design

Realistic 3D visualizations and rendering of solar installations on residential and commercial structures using state-of-the-art CAD models.

Solar 2D Layout Design

Highly precise module placement layout drawings specifying row spacings, tilt angles, pitch, and structural boundaries.

Shadow Analysis

Advanced shade modeling software simulating yearly solar paths to identify objects obstructing solar irradiance and energy production.

SLD Drawings

Electrical Single Line Diagrams highlighting details of PV modules, string configurations, inverters, combiners, and grid connections.

BOQ Preparation

Complete Bills of Quantities summarizing all structural and electrical components required, preventing material ordering leaks.

CEIG Drawings

Electrical Inspector approval drawings, lightning protection calculations, and grounding layouts compliant with CEIG regulatory standards.
