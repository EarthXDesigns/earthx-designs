About EarthX Designs

A trusted technical design partner for top-tier solar developers, EPCs, and installation professionals worldwide.

Our Story

Pioneering Solar Engineering Solutions

Founded by a group of passionate solar engineers, EarthX Designs has grown to become a leading drafting and technical documentation agency. We recognized that fast-growing solar installers often struggle with engineering bottlenecks, permit delays, and non-compliance drafting cycles. We set out to solve this.

Today, we provide a full-spectrum engineering back-office for solar companies. By outsourcing drafting, electrical routing designs, and structural calculations to us, our clients speed up installation cycles, lower overhead, and increase permit approval rates.

From small residential layouts to massive multi-megawatt ground-mount arrays with tracking systems, our designs are optimized for field buildability and local codes.

Core Principles

Our Operational Standards

Every engineering layout that leaves our office goes through a strict quality checklist to ensure zero errors in the field.

Regulatory Compliance

We stay updated on NEC code changes, local municipal permit guidelines, and utility interconnect requirements to ensure your plans are approved on first submission.

Engineering Optimization

We don't just draft; we optimize. We analyze inverter loading ratios, string lengths, cable sizing, and structural racking configurations to optimize balance-of-system (BOS) costs.

Agile Turnaround

Solar installations operate on tight deadlines. Our design pipeline is structured to return draft files in 24 to 48 hours, keeping your installation crews active in the field.
